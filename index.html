I’m Sonet, a Software Engineering student at Daffodil International University in Bangladesh. I’m drawn to the moment when something complicated finally makes sense—and even more to turning that understanding into something useful.

My interests sit at the intersection of software, data, and intelligent systems. Whether I’m working through an algorithm or exploring a dataset, I like asking: “What can this help us understand or do better?”
